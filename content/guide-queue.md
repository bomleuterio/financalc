# Weekly guide queue

The weekly drafting routine takes the first unchecked topic, drafts it as a pull request,
and checks it off in the same PR. Reorder, edit, or add topics freely. Each topic names the
calculator it should link to and anything that must be sourced rather than assumed.

- [ ] How much house can you afford? Payment-to-income rules (28/36) with worked examples — links: mortgage
- [ ] How long should a car loan be? 36 vs. 48 vs. 60 vs. 72 months, total cost and negative equity — links: auto-loan
- [ ] Student loans: pay off early or invest? — links: student-loan; check current federal repayment plans on studentaid.gov
- [ ] Capital gains basics: holding periods, the 0% bracket, and tax-loss harvesting — links: capital-gains; 2026 thresholds from IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32
- [ ] Budgeting methods compared: 50/30/20, zero-based, and pay-yourself-first — links: budget
- [ ] Inflation and your savings: real returns explained — links: inflation, savings
- [ ] Debt consolidation loans: when they save money and when they don't — links: personal-loan, debt-payoff
- [ ] How to calculate and track your net worth — links: net-worth; no "net worth by age" figures unless from a cited survey (e.g. Federal Reserve SCF)
- [ ] CD ladders vs. high-yield savings: how each works — links: cd, savings; no current-rate claims, use clearly hypothetical rates
- [ ] Health Savings Accounts in 2026: the triple tax advantage — links: income-tax; 2026 HSA limits must be verified from IRS sources
- [ ] When to claim Social Security: 62 vs. full retirement age vs. 70 — links: retirement; reduction/credit percentages must be verified from ssa.gov
